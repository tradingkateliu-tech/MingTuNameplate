Company Profile

Your Custom Metal Nameplate Partner

MingTu Nameplate (Foshan) Technology Co., Ltd. is a professional manufacturer specializing in the research, production, and customization of metal nameplates. With years of industry experience, our company is located in Hegui Industrial Park, Heshun, Lishui Town, Nanhai District, Foshan City, Guangdong, China.

We operate standardized production workshops and a complete production line, with equipment including a 110-ton stamping press, automatic punching machines, a tunnel oven, printing presses, a UV curing machine, and precision screen-printing equipment. We provide multi-process manufacturing using aluminum, zinc alloy, stainless steel, nickel, and other metal materials.

Our products serve fragrance and cosmetics, electronic devices and accessories, cultural and creative gifts, and other industries. We work with customers to develop custom metal label solutions for their products and brands.
